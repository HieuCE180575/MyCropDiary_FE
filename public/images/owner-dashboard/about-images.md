# About page image assets

All five JPGs were created for this project with the built-in ImageGen tool, then encoded as JPEG. They are synthetic illustrations in a photographic style, not photographs of actual customers or team members. Existing Owner Dashboard assets are unchanged.

Prompt set (photorealistic-natural; no text, logos, watermarks, illustration or 3D):

- `about-hero.jpg`: Wide 2:1 editorial photograph of a Vietnamese farmer in a conical hat and blue plaid shirt using a smartphone on the right side of a vegetable garden; open garden on the left, bamboo trellises, wooded hills, natural morning sunlight, anatomically correct hands.
- `about-story.jpg`: Landscape 3:2 over-the-shoulder view of a Vietnamese farmer in a conical hat and green work shirt writing crop notes in a notebook beside vegetable rows; natural hands, soft golden sunlight and realistic textures.
- `about-farmer.jpg`: Vertical 3:4 portrait of a middle-aged Vietnamese farm owner in an olive sun hat and beige work shirt, calm expression, vegetable farm, natural morning light.
- `about-manager.jpg`: Vertical 3:4 portrait of a young Vietnamese farm manager with short black hair and glasses, green polo shirt, using a tablet at a vegetable farm in natural sunlight.
- `about-worker.jpg`: Vertical 3:4 photograph of a Vietnamese worker in a conical hat and blue plaid shirt tending vegetable plants; side view, natural pose, golden daylight.

The two small decorative assets, `about-leaves.svg` and `about-landscape.svg`, are original vector drawings created in code for the mission and vision cards.
